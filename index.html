Product

White-Label Reviews Hub

A product we are building for local marketing agencies: manage every client's Google reviews from one platform. It is also exactly the kind of custom web app work we take on.

The system pulls Google Business Profile reviews for every client business into one shared inbox, drafts AI replies, and requires the business owner to approve the exact reply through a secure magic link before anything is published. It does not auto-publish, and it does not gate reviews.

  • ✓ Multi-tenant, white-label: each agency gets its own workspace with role-based permissions and strict data isolation between clients.
  • ✓ One review inbox: reviews sync from every client location, filterable by rating, reply status, and location, with risk flags on the reviews that need attention first.
  • ✓ AI reply drafts: replies generated in the client's brand voice and saved as immutable versions, so every change leaves a trace.
  • ✓ Owner approval: nothing goes live until the business owner confirms it through a secure link — the agency cannot publish on their behalf.

The product is still being finished and is not released. We are not taking customers until the approval and publishing workflow is fully tested.

How We Work

Scoped and quoted individually for every project

We don't sell fixed packages, because two businesses with the same page count rarely need the same work. Every project runs through four stages.

  1. 01

    Discovery & scoping

    We learn about your business, your customers, and what the site actually needs to do. The first consultation is free and carries no obligation.

  2. 02

    Written proposal

    You get a written scope, a fixed price, and a milestone timeline before any work starts. No surprises later.

  3. 03

    Design & build

    We design and develop in stages and share progress continuously, so your feedback lands while changes are still inexpensive.

  4. 04

    Launch & handover

    We deploy to production, connect your domain, and hand over the accounts with documentation. Ongoing support after handover is optional.
